Exterior painting

A paint job that takes one for the house

Exterior paint is weather armor. In this climate, the difference between five years and two is all in the prep.

What an exterior job covers

  • Siding — painted or stained, lap or panel
  • Trim, fascia and soffits, cut in clean
  • Porch, deck and railings — paint or stain, wood grain left visible on stain jobs
  • Front door and entry — the part every guest measures the house by
  • Fences and gates on the property line

On a repaint, nothing gets paint until it’s been washed down, scraped, spot-patched, caulked at the gaps and primed where the substrate is bare. Loose boards get fixed before they get coated — repairs are on the crew’s list too, so the fix happens in the same visit.

Built for this weather

Vancouver gets its rain on schedule, and the Willamette never politely waits for a fresh coat to cure. A well-prepped exterior keeps moisture out of the equation: caulked seams, primed bare wood, paint laid in dry windows at the thickness the film needs. Wood siding? Stain and sealing work the same discipline — prep first, then finish that breathes.
