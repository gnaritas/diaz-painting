How a job runs

Four steps, same order every time

1

You tell us the space

A call or the contact form — rooms, surfaces, what you’re picturing. We talk it through.

2

We prep

Floors and fixtures covered, hardware pulled, cracks patched, gaps caulked, bare spots primed.

3

We paint

Cut lines by hand, walls rolled evenly, trim brushed clean — coats laid at the pace the finish needs.

4

We finish

Tape out, hardware back on, touch-ups done, and the space walked together before we pack up.
